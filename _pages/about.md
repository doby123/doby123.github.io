Professional Experience
======
I joined [Clemson University](https://www.clemson.edu/) as an Assistant Professor in 2025. I had been working as Project Scientist at NSF National Center for Atmospheric Research/[High Altitude Observatory](https://www2.hao.ucar.edu/) from 2022 to 2025, and as a postdoctoral researcher there from 2019 to 2022. I obtained my PhD degree from [Virginia Tech](https://www.space.vt.edu/). My PhD dissertation title was "[Solar Wind-Magnetosphere-Ionosphere Coupling: Multiscale Study with Computational Models](https://vtechworks.lib.vt.edu/bitstreams/3babf7f8-9b8c-4508-aa16-744e00714579/download)".

I have been serving as a [Working Group Lead](https://cgs.jhuapl.edu/Our-Team/index.php#sec_5) at the NASA CGS Modeling Group, and as a committee member on the NASA Living With a Star ([LWS](https://lws.gsfc.nasa.gov/)) Program Analysis Group since 2024, and a co-lead of the NSF Geospace Environment Modeling Focus Group of Magnetospheric Sources of Particle Precipitation and Their Role on Electrodynamic Coupling of Magnetosphere-Ionosphere-Thermosphere Systems ([MPEC](https://gem.epss.ucla.edu/mediawiki/index.php/FG:_Magnetospheric_Sources_of_Particle_Precipitation_and_Their_Role_on_Electrodynamic_Coupling_of_Magnetosphere-Ionosphere-Thermosphere_Systems)) since 2022.
